# What we do
Welcome to the Lab Manual for the Information Ecosystems Lab (InfoEco). 

Our lab investigates the structure and dynamics of information ecosystems. An ecosystem is a social system that meets the following criteria:
- Actors must **exchange information**. Just as natural ecosystems function around the exchange of energy, information ecosystems are those centered around the distribution of information.
- It must be **massive**, think millions of actors, not just dozens. 
- It must be **complex**, with non-trivial social structure and heterogeneous actors

Its a broad category. The following can be considered information ecosystems:
- The global institution of science
- A social media website, such as Reddit
- The official news
- An open source software community

An example of relevant questions:
- How does information spread and how do differences in social structure affect it? How do scientists find relevant work, 
- How do new LLM technologies affect their ability to stay up-to-date with the literature? - To what extent do malicious agents, such as misinformation bots, shape the discourse on social media? Questions like these are what we aim to understand. 
- Are there any types of institutions or technical changes that can foster a healthier information ecosystem, one where verified information can reach where it is needed most?
